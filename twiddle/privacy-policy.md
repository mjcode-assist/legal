# Twiddle — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** October 16, 2026

Twiddle ("the app") is a fidget-toy app for iOS and Android, published under the **MJ Code** brand by an independent developer, whose name appears on the app's App Store and Google Play listings ("we", "us").

This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short on purpose: Twiddle has no account, no sign-in, and collects as little as it can.

## In short

- No account, no email, no name, no location, no contacts, no advertising.
- Anonymous **usage analytics** so we can understand which toys people enjoy and fix problems.
- **Session replay** for a random **20 %** of sessions: a visual recording of the app screens during that session, kept for 30 days.
- Your **settings and progress** are stored on your device (and in your own iCloud / Google Drive space once cloud backup is available).
- **Purchases** are handled by Apple and Google. We never see your payment details.

## Data we collect

### 1. Usage analytics

We use **PostHog**, an analytics service, to record events such as: the app being opened, a toy being used, a toy being unlocked, the settings being changed, a pack being viewed or purchased.

Each event comes with:

- a **random anonymous identifier** generated on your device (not linked to your name, email or Apple/Google account);
- technical information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, screen size, time zone;
- an approximate country derived from your network connection (no precise location).

**Why:** to understand how the app is used, measure which toys are popular, detect crashes and bugs, and improve the app. **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in maintaining and improving the app.

### 2. Session replay

For a random **20 % of sessions**, PostHog also records a **visual replay**: periodic screenshots of the app's screens while you use it, together with the taps that produced them. This lets us see, for example, that a gesture does not work as intended on a given phone.

- Replays only show the app itself (the toys, settings and purchase screens). The app does not display any personal data, and the two "form" toys do not store or transmit what you type.
- Replays are linked to the same anonymous identifier as analytics, never to your identity.
- Replays are **kept for 30 days**, then deleted automatically.

**Why:** to find and fix usability problems. **Legal basis:** our legitimate interest in improving the app. If you would rather not be included, see "Your choices" below.

### 3. Settings and progress

Which toys you have enabled, their order, which toys you have unlocked, your usage counter, your theme and appearance choices. This data is stored **on your device**. When cloud backup becomes available, a copy will be stored in **your own iCloud account** (iOS) or **Google Drive app data** (Android), so that you find your progress again on a new phone. We cannot access that cloud copy; it belongs to your Apple or Google account.

### 4. Purchases

Packs of toys are sold as in-app purchases through **Apple App Store** and **Google Play**. Apple or Google process the payment and tell the app which packs you own. We receive **no payment or card details** and no billing identity. Your purchase history is managed by Apple or Google and can be restored from the app ("Restore purchases").

## Data we do not collect

We do **not** collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. The app contains **no advertising** and **no third-party ad trackers**. We do not sell data.

## Service providers

| Provider | Role | Where data is stored |
|---|---|---|
| PostHog (PostHog Inc.) | Analytics and session replay | European Union (EU cloud) |
| Apple | App distribution, in-app purchases, iCloud backup | Per Apple's own policies |
| Google | App distribution, in-app purchases, Google Drive backup | Per Google's own policies |

Analytics and replay data are stored in the European Union and are not transferred outside the EU by us.

## Retention

- Session replays: **30 days**.
- Analytics events: kept while the app is maintained, in aggregated or anonymous form, to compare versions over time.
- Settings and progress: on your device until you delete the app or reset progression; in your own cloud account until you delete it there.

## Children

Twiddle is suitable for all ages and is not directed specifically at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, and the anonymous data described above is collected the same way for every user.

## Your choices and rights

- **Session replay and analytics:** you can refuse all data collection by not using the app; an in-app opt-out switch is not available yet. Uninstalling the app stops all collection immediately.
- **Progress data:** "Reset progression" in the app erases it on your device (and in your cloud copy when applicable).
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. Because our data is anonymous, we may not be able to link a request to a specific record, but we will do our best. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights or ask any question, write to **mj.code.assist+twiddle@gmail.com**.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example when cloud backup launches). The current version is always available at the address published on the app's store listings, and the effective date above tells you when it was last changed.
