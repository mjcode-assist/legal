# Twiddle — Support

Twiddle is a fidget-toy app for iOS and Android, published under the **MJ Code** brand.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, ideas: write to **mj.code.assist+twiddle@gmail.com**. We usually answer within a few days.

To help us fix a problem quickly, please include:

- your phone model and its operating system version (for example "iPhone 13, iOS 18.1");
- what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead;
- a screenshot or screen recording, if you have one.

## Frequently asked questions

**I bought a pack but the toys are still locked.**
Open the Settings tab (or the purchase screen) and tap **Restore purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used for the purchase. If it still does not work, contact us with the date of the purchase.

**Can I get a refund?**
Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds go through them: [Apple refund request](https://support.apple.com/118223) or [Google Play refund request](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

**How do I start over?**
Delete the app and install it again. Your unlocked toys, usage counter, toy order and theme are stored on your device only, so this brings the app back to its first-launch state. Purchased packs can always be restored.

**Do I need an account?**
No. Twiddle has no account, no sign-in and no personal profile. There is nothing to delete on our side. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy) for what the app collects.

**Will my progress follow me to a new phone?**
Not yet. A backup to your own iCloud or Google Drive space is planned.
